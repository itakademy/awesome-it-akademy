# Contributing to Awesome IT-Akademy

Thank you for your interest in contributing! This list is maintained for **IT-Akademy students** and the wider developer community.

All contributions are welcome — whether it's a new resource, a fix, or a suggestion.

---

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork** this repository
2. **Create a branch** for your contribution (`git checkout -b add-my-resource`)
3. **Add your resource** following the guidelines below
4. **Commit** your changes (`git commit -m "Add [resource name]"`)
5. **Push** to your branch (`git push origin add-my-resource`)
6. **Open a Pull Request** with a clear description of what you added and why

---

## Guidelines

### Content Quality

- The resource must be **high quality** and **relevant** to developers, cybersecurity specialists, or tech students
- No spam, affiliate links, or self-promotion
- The resource should provide **real educational value**
- Prefer **free** or **freemium** resources when possible

### Formatting

- Add links using the format: `- https://example.com – Short description`
- Place your link in the **appropriate existing category**
- Keep descriptions **concise** (a few words)
- Maintain **alphabetical order** within each section when possible

### What We Accept

- Documentation and reference sites
- Learning platforms and tutorials
- Open-source tools and projects
- Books, podcasts, and YouTube channels
- Tech blogs and news sources

### What We Do Not Accept

- Paid-only resources without significant free content
- Low-quality or outdated content
- Duplicate links already in the list
- Content not related to tech or software development

---

## Suggesting a New Category

If your resource doesn't fit an existing category, feel free to **suggest a new one** in your Pull Request. Provide a brief justification for why the category is needed.

---

## Reporting Issues

Found a broken link or outdated resource? [Open an issue](../../issues/new) with:

- The broken or outdated link
- A suggested replacement (if you have one)

---

## Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. This project follows general open-source etiquette:

- Be welcoming to newcomers
- Provide constructive feedback
- Respect differing viewpoints

---

Thank you for helping make this list better for everyone! 🚀
